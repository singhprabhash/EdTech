# Supplied content: verification required before production

The current implementation renders user-supplied design copy in an unindexed local
preview. This document does not approve its factual accuracy. No metadata has been
invented to fill missing catalogue pages.

## Source reconciliation

- Catalogue: 10 cards; Horticulture page: six cards; detailed page: one Frontier
  Agronomy specimen and three additional related cards. The generated source index
  therefore contains 19 card-derived records, including separately retained editions.
- Home/catalogue describe Frontier Agronomy with ISBN `978-93-94572-88-1`; the
  supplied detail uses hardcover `978-93-94821-45-2` and paperback `978-93-94821-46-9`.
  The detail price, biography, DOI and claims are specimen copy, not proof of identity.
- Tropical Fruit Cultivation has different authors/year/ISBN in catalogue versus
  Horticulture. Separate routes retain the supplied editions; do not merge by title.
- Source totals include 46+, 15+, nine Horticulture titles and 140+ related titles.
  These are unreconciled supplied claims. Interactive counts use actual available
  cards, and nonexistent pagination is disabled. No extra books are fabricated.
- Accreditation, ICAR/UGC alignment, peer review, discounts, delivery, author awards,
  ORCID/DOI, empirical trial statistics and service turnaround claims need approval.
- Missing policy, agreement, sample, TOC and guidelines files have not been created
  or represented as successful downloads. Request actions replace unavailable files.

## Release gate

Obtain one owner-approved canonical record per edition with subject/format/price
provenance, approved policy/download files, verified business contacts and legal
copy. Reconcile the detail specimen with its catalogue record, then replace preview
inputs through a validated import. Only after content and operational gates pass
should indexing, sitemap/schema, deployment and submission features be enabled.
